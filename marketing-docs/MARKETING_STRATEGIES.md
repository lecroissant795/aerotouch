# AeroTouch Marketing Strategies to Test

## Overview
This document outlines data-driven marketing strategies to test for AeroTouch insoles and recovery products. Each strategy includes implementation details, success metrics, and testing frameworks.

---

## 1. Paid Acquisition Channels

### Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)
**Target Audiences:**
- Athletes & runners (25-45, interest in marathons, CrossFit, trail running)
- People with foot pain conditions (plantar fasciitis, flat feet, high arches)
- Healthcare workers & service industry (nurses, retail, hospitality)
- Fitness enthusiasts following recovery/wellness accounts

**Creative Angles to Test:**
- Before/after pain relief testimonials
- Product demonstration videos (acupressure points, magnetic therapy)
- User-generated content from athletes
- Educational content about foot health
- Bundle kit value propositions

**Campaign Structure:**
- Cold traffic: Awareness campaigns with video views
- Warm traffic: Retargeting site visitors with dynamic product ads
- Hot traffic: Cart abandoners with discount incentives

**Budget Allocation:** Start with $50-100/day, scale winners
**Success Metrics:** CAC < $25, ROAS > 2.5x

### Google Ads
**Search Campaigns:**
- High-intent keywords: "best insoles for plantar fasciitis", "magnetic therapy insoles", "foot pain relief"
- Competitor keywords: "[competitor name] alternative"
- Problem-aware: "how to relieve foot pain", "standing all day foot pain"

**Shopping Campaigns:**
- Product feed optimization with high-quality images
- Competitive pricing display
- Promotional annotations (free shipping, money-back guarantee)

**YouTube Pre-Roll:**
- 15-30 second product demos
- Customer testimonial compilations
- Educational content about foot biomechanics

**Budget Allocation:** $75-150/day
**Success Metrics:** Search CPA < $30, Shopping ROAS > 3x

### TikTok Ads
**Content Strategy:**
- Unboxing and first impressions
- "Day in the life" with AeroTouch insoles
- Pain relief transformation stories
- Comparison with drugstore insoles
- Behind-the-scenes manufacturing/quality

**Targeting:**
- Fitness & wellness enthusiasts
- Healthcare workers
- People engaging with pain relief content
- Ages 25-54

**Budget Allocation:** $30-75/day (lower cost platform)
**Success Metrics:** CTR > 2%, CPA < $20

## 4. Email Marketing & Retention

### Welcome Series (5 emails)
1. **Day 0:** Welcome + 10% discount code
2. **Day 2:** Educational content about foot health
3. **Day 5:** Customer testimonials and social proof
4. **Day 7:** Product recommendations based on pain points
5. **Day 10:** Urgency email (discount expiring)

### Abandoned Cart Series (3 emails)
1. **1 hour:** Reminder with product images
2. **24 hours:** Social proof + free shipping offer
3. **48 hours:** Final urgency (limited stock, discount expiring)

### Post-Purchase Series
1. **Day 1:** Order confirmation + what to expect
2. **Day 3:** Shipping notification + usage tips
3. **Day 14:** How to get the most from your insoles
4. **Day 30:** Review request + referral incentive
5. **Day 60:** Replenishment reminder + bundle offer

### Segmentation Strategy
- By product purchased (insoles vs accessories)
- By pain point (plantar fasciitis vs general comfort)
- By engagement level (active vs dormant)
- By purchase frequency (one-time vs repeat)

**Success Metrics:** Open rate > 25%, click rate > 4%, email revenue 20% of total

## 5. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

### A/B Tests to Run

**Homepage:**
- Hero headline variations (pain-focused vs benefit-focused)
- CTA button copy ("Shop Now" vs "Find Relief" vs "Get Started")
- Social proof placement (above vs below fold)
- Video autoplay vs static image

**Product Pages:**
- Price anchoring (show compare-at price vs hide)
- Review placement (top vs bottom)
- Add to cart vs Buy Now button prominence
- Size guide accessibility
- Bundle offers positioning

**Cart:**
- Free shipping threshold messaging
- Upsell product selection
- Timer urgency (5 min vs 10 min vs no timer)
- Trust badges placement

**Checkout:**
- Guest checkout vs account required
- Progress indicator style
- Payment method order
- Discount code field visibility

**Success Metrics:** Lift > 5% on conversion rate, statistical significance > 95%

## 10. Testing Framework

### Prioritization Matrix
Evaluate each test on:
- **Impact:** Potential revenue increase (1-10)
- **Confidence:** Likelihood of success (1-10)
- **Ease:** Implementation difficulty (1-10, higher = easier)

**Formula:** Priority Score = (Impact × Confidence) / Ease

### Testing Cadence
- Run 2-3 A/B tests simultaneously
- Minimum 2-week test duration
- Require 95% statistical significance
- Document all learnings in shared database

### Success Metrics Dashboard
**Acquisition:**
- Traffic sources and volume
- Cost per click (CPC)
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)

**Conversion:**
- Conversion rate by channel
- Average order value (AOV)
- Cart abandonment rate
- Checkout completion rate

**Retention:**
- Repeat purchase rate
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Churn rate
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)

**Overall:**
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target > 3:1)
- Gross margin

## Key Takeaways

1. **Start small, scale winners** - Test multiple channels with modest budgets, then double down on what works
2. **Focus on pain points** - Your customers are buying relief, not just insoles
3. **Build trust fast** - Money-back guarantee, reviews, and social proof are critical
4. **Optimize for LTV** - Email marketing and retention are more profitable than acquisition
5. **Test everything** - Never assume, always validate with data
6. **Track religiously** - Use UTM parameters, unique discount codes, and proper attribution
