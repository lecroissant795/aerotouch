## Threats & Mitigation

### Threat 1: Increased Competition
**Risk:** More DTC insole brands entering market
**Mitigation:**
- Build strong brand loyalty
- Focus on customer experience
- Continuous product innovation
- Expand product line

### Threat 2: Price Pressure
**Risk:** Competitors lowering prices
**Mitigation:**
- Emphasize value, not just price
- Bundle offerings
- Loyalty program
- Focus on therapeutic benefits

### Threat 3: Retail Disruption
**Risk:** Amazon or big-box retailers launching private label
**Mitigation:**
- Build direct customer relationships
- Create brand community
- Focus on education and content
- Offer superior customer service

### Threat 4: Economic Downturn
**Risk:** Consumers cutting discretionary spending
**Mitigation:**
- Emphasize health investment
- Offer payment plans
- Create budget-friendly options
- Focus on pain relief (necessity)
