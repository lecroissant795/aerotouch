## Social Media Content Calendar Template

### Weekly Posting Schedule

**Monday: Educational**
- Topic: Foot health tips, pain prevention, biomechanics
- Format: Carousel post or infographic
- Example: "5 Signs You Need Better Insoles"

**Tuesday: Product Feature**
- Topic: Highlight specific product benefits
- Format: Product photo + detailed caption
- Example: "How Magnetic Therapy Reduces Inflammation"

**Wednesday: User-Generated Content**
- Topic: Customer testimonials and transformations
- Format: Repost customer content (with permission)
- Example: Before/after stories, reviews

**Thursday: Behind-the-Scenes**
- Topic: Company culture, manufacturing, team
- Format: Stories or Reels
- Example: "How AeroTouch Insoles Are Made"

**Friday: Engagement**
- Topic: Questions, polls, interactive content
- Format: Stories with polls/questions
- Example: "What's your biggest foot pain struggle?"

**Saturday: Lifestyle**
- Topic: Product in use, aspirational content
- Format: High-quality lifestyle photography
- Example: "Weekend adventures start with comfortable feet"

**Sunday: Community**
- Topic: Celebrate customers, share wins
- Format: Testimonial compilation or spotlight
- Example: "This week's pain-free heroes"

---

*Use these templates as starting points—always test and optimize based on your audience's response.*
