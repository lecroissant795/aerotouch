# AeroTouch Customer Retention & Lifecycle Marketing

## Customer Lifecycle Stages

### Stage 1: Awareness (Pre-Customer)
**Goal:** Capture attention and build interest
**Duration:** First interaction to email signup
**Key Metrics:** Traffic, email signups, social followers

**Tactics:**
- Content marketing (blog posts, social media)
- Paid advertising (Meta, Google, TikTok)
- Influencer partnerships
- SEO optimization
- PR and media coverage

---

### Stage 2: Consideration (Lead)
**Goal:** Educate and build trust
**Duration:** Email signup to first purchase
**Key Metrics:** Email engagement, site visits, time on site

**Tactics:**
- Welcome email series (5 emails over 10 days)
- Educational content about foot health
- Social proof (reviews, testimonials)
- Retargeting ads
- Discount incentives

**Email Sequence:**
```
Day 0: Welcome + 10% discount
Day 2: Educational content (foot pain causes)
Day 5: Customer testimonials
Day 7: Product recommendations
Day 10: Urgency (discount expiring)
```

---

### Stage 3: Purchase (New Customer)
**Goal:** Deliver exceptional first experience
**Duration:** First purchase to product delivery
**Key Metrics:** Order completion rate, delivery time, support tickets

**Tactics:**
- Order confirmation email
- Shipping updates
- Usage instructions
- Proactive customer support
- Unboxing experience optimization

**Email Sequence:**
```
Hour 0: Order confirmation + what to expect
Day 1: Shipping notification + tracking
Day 3: Delivery reminder + usage tips
Day 7: Check-in (how are they working?)
```

---

### Stage 4: Onboarding (Active Customer)
**Goal:** Ensure product satisfaction and engagement
**Duration:** Product delivery to 30 days
**Key Metrics:** Product usage, satisfaction score, support tickets

**Tactics:**
- Usage tips and best practices
- Educational content
- Check-in emails
- Early feedback collection
- Proactive support

**Email Sequence:**
```
Day 1: Welcome to AeroTouch family
Day 3: How to get the most from your insoles
Day 7: Common questions answered
Day 14: Are you feeling relief?
Day 21: Tips for long-term care
Day 30: Review request + referral offer
```

---

### Stage 5: Retention (Repeat Customer)
**Goal:** Drive repeat purchases and increase LTV
**Duration:** 30 days to 12 months
**Key Metrics:** Repeat purchase rate, AOV, customer satisfaction

**Tactics:**
- Replenishment reminders
- Cross-sell and upsell campaigns
- Loyalty program
- Exclusive offers
- VIP treatment

**Email Campaigns:**
```
Day 60: Replenishment reminder (insoles wear out)
Day 90: Cross-sell accessories (massage roller, socks)
Day 120: Bundle offer (complete recovery kit)
Day 180: Loyalty program invitation
Day 270: Win-back campaign (if no purchase)
Day 365: Anniversary celebration + special offer
```

---

### Stage 6: Advocacy (Brand Champion)
**Goal:** Turn customers into advocates
**Duration:** Ongoing
**Key Metrics:** Referrals, reviews, social mentions, NPS

**Tactics:**
- Referral program (give $15, get $15)
- Review incentives
- User-generated content campaigns
- VIP community
- Early access to new products

**Engagement:**
```
- Monthly: Exclusive content for VIPs
- Quarterly: New product previews
- Ongoing: Referral rewards
- Ongoing: Social media features
```

## Customer Segmentation Strategies

### Segment 1: High-Value Customers
**Definition:** 3+ purchases or $200+ LTV
**Size:** ~10% of customer base
**Revenue:** ~40% of total revenue

**Strategy:**
- VIP program enrollment
- Exclusive early access
- Personalized outreach
- Premium support
- Special gifts and surprises

---

### Segment 2: Repeat Customers
**Definition:** 2 purchases, $50-200 LTV
**Size:** ~20% of customer base
**Revenue:** ~35% of total revenue

**Strategy:**
- Loyalty program engagement
- Cross-sell campaigns
- Referral incentives
- Exclusive offers
- Community building

---

### Segment 3: One-Time Customers
**Definition:** 1 purchase, any value
**Size:** ~60% of customer base
**Revenue:** ~20% of total revenue

**Strategy:**
- Replenishment reminders
- Educational content
- Second purchase incentives
- Product recommendations
- Re-engagement campaigns

---

### Segment 4: Cart Abandoners
**Definition:** Added to cart but didn't purchase
**Size:** Variable
**Conversion:** 10-30% with proper follow-up

**Strategy:**
- 3-email abandoned cart sequence
- Discount incentives
- Social proof
- Free shipping offers
- Urgency messaging

---

### Segment 5: Email Subscribers (Non-Customers)
**Definition:** Signed up but never purchased
**Size:** Variable
**Conversion:** 5-15% over 90 days

**Strategy:**
- Welcome series
- Educational content
- Social proof
- Limited-time offers
- Retargeting ads
